I love working on the visual and interactive aspects of applications, bringing ideas to life through design and code. That’s what excites me the most! Since then, I’ve been dedicating myself to mastering front-end technologies, honing my skills in: I’m always eager to learn, grow, and create meaningful digital experiences.
